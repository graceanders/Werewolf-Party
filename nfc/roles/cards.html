Werewolf role tags

One card per role. Make one tag per player you expect, using the recommended mix from the Game Master screen.

Cupid

On the first night, choose two players (you may choose yourself) to fall in love. They learn each other's names, and if one dies, the other dies of heartbreak.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/cupid

Seer

Each night you learn one player's true role. Guide the village without painting a target on yourself.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/seer

Werewolf

Each night the wolves agree on one villager to eat. By day, blend in. You win when wolves equal or outnumber the village.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/werewolf

Doctor

Each night choose one player to protect from the wolves. You may not protect the same player two nights running.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/doctor

Witch

You have one potion that saves the wolves' victim and one that kills any player. Each can be used once per game.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/witch

Hunter

If you die, for any reason, you immediately take one other player down with you.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/hunter

Villager

You have no special power. Listen, argue and vote out the werewolves before they eat the village.

https://graceanders.github.io/Werewolf-Party/#/r/villager
